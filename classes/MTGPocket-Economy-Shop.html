Shop
in package
  • Application

Shop.php : 41

The points shop: players sell cards they don't want for points, and spend points on single cards or on packs of one color of one set.

Prices come from a PriceList. Cards a player's decks use are never sold: a sale only takes copies beyond what any one deck needs.

Every purchase and sale holds the player's lock and then their inventory's (the order daily packs use), and writes the points only after the cards, so a failure never takes points without cards or cards without points.
