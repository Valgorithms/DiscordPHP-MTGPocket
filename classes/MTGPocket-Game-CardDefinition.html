CardDefinition.php : 29

What the rules need to know about a card, read from its pool data: its cost, types, power and toughness, keywords, mana abilities and the spell effects the engine understands.

Rules text is read with TextParser; any part of it the engine does not understand yet is listed in $unsupported so players can be told, instead of it silently doing nothing.
