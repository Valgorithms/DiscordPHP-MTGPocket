DeckBuilder
in package
  • Application

DeckBuilder.php : 43

Builds and edits a player's decks and side decks from the cards they own.

A deck may use each card the player owns as many times as they own it, main and side deck together; the same cards can go in any number of decks. Basic lands are free and unlimited (BasicLands).

Which cards a format allows, and the deck sizes it needs, are checked when a match starts, not here.

Mistakes the player can fix (no such deck, not enough copies, …) are thrown as InvalidArgumentException or OutOfBoundsException with a message to show them.
