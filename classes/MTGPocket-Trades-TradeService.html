TradeService
in package
  • Application

TradeService.php : 40

Trades between players. One player offers cards and points for cards and points of another's; the other accepts or declines. Sending the offer is the sender's confirmation and accepting it is the other player's, so a trade happens only when both have agreed to the same revision of it.

Nothing is held back while an offer is open. When it is accepted, both sides are checked again (the cards owned, not needed by a deck, and the points) and the whole trade happens at once, under the offer's lock, both players' locks and both inventories' locks, always taken in the same order, so a trade never half happens and two trades never trip over each other.
