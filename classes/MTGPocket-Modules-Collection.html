in package
  • Application
implements Module uses InteractionTrait, PocketTrait

FinalYes
Collection.php : 40

`/collection [set] [color] [rarity] [name] [player]`: the cards a player owns, in pages, with a picker that opens any of them in DiscordPHP-MTG's card view.

The pages are CollectionPages. This module also opens the cards picked from any of the game's messages (pocket:card).
