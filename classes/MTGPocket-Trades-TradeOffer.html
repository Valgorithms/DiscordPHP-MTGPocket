TradeOffer
in package
  • Application

FinalYes
TradeOffer.php : 28

A trade one player offers another: cards and points they give, for cards and points they want back. The offer is open until the other player accepts or declines it, its sender cancels it, or it expires.

Every change to an open offer bumps its revision, so an Accept click on an older copy of the offer never agrees to something different.
