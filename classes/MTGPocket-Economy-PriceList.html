PriceList
in package

PriceList.php : 28

What cards and packs cost in points, and what selling a card pays, from the table in `config/economy.php`.

A card's price is its rarity's price times its set's age multiplier, so rarer cards and newer sets cost more. Selling pays a fixed share of the buy price, under 1, so points cannot be made by selling and buying back.
