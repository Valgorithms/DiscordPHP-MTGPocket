DailyPacks
in package
  • Application

DailyPacks.php : 35

One free pack a day per player. The day turns over at midnight UTC, so every player's next pack is ready at the same moment.

A player picks the set and color of their pack, or leaves either to chance. Any imported set can be picked, in any color that has a rare or mythic rare to fill the rare slot.
