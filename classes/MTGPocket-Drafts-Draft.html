Draft
in package
  • Application

FinalYes
Draft.php : 31

A booster draft event: a pod of players drafting packs of one set, then building decks and playing Swiss rounds with them.

It moves through SIGNUP, DRAFTING, BUILDING and PLAYING to OVER, or is CANCELLED before it starts.

While drafting, each seat has a queue of packs passed to it; it takes a card from the first and passes the rest on. A pairing is {a, b, match, result}: b is null for a bye, match the game's id once it has started, and result one of a, b or draw once it is decided.
