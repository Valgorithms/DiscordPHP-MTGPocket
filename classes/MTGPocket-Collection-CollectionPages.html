in package

FinalYes
CollectionPages.php : 34

Pages of a player's collection, filtered by set, color, rarity and name.

A page's buttons carry the whole query in their custom id (pocket:page:<query>:<page>), so they keep working after a restart. Under the page sit Filter (a form for the query) and Menu.
