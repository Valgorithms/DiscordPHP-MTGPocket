MatchService
in package
  • Application

FinalYes
MatchService.php : 47

Matches: direct challenges, the matchmaking queue and the games they start. Each player is in at most one live match (a challenge or a game) and at most one queue at a time.

Every match is played in a game mode, with the main deck of each player's active deck, rental deck, or the one they name: the deck has to follow the mode's rules and library, and hold only cards the player still owns (a rental needs a rental game left for the day instead). Matchmaking pairs players waiting in the same mode whose ratings are close (a range that widens the longer someone waits); those games are ranked, move both players on the mode's Ladder and pay points (see MatchRewards).
