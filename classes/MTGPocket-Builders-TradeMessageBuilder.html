in package
  • Application

TradeMessageBuilder.php : 38

Trade messages: an open offer with **Accept**, **Decline** and **Cancel**, a finished one, and a player's open offers.

Custom ids: pocket:trade:<id>:<accept|decline|cancel>:<revision> on an offer, and pocket:tradeview for the picker that opens one.
