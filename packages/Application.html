Table of Contents

Classes

DraftMessageBuilder
Booster draft messages: a pod and its standings, a player's pack to pick from, their picks and deck, and the pod's announcements.
MatchMessageBuilder
The messages of a match:
MenuMessageBuilder
The game's menus: the home panel `/menu` opens, and the buttons and menus every panel uses to get around.
PocketMessageBuilder
The game's messages, as Components V2 in the style of DiscordPHP-MTG's: an opened pack, the packs on offer, a deck and a player's decks.
ShopMessageBuilder
The shop's messages: a player's points and the price list, a card's price, what a sale or purchase did, and the cards a bulk sale would sell.
TradeMessageBuilder
Trade messages: an open offer with **Accept**, **Decline** and **Cancel**, a finished one, and a player's open offers.
BasicLands
Basic lands. Packs never hold them (card pools leave them out), so every player may put as many as they like in a deck without owning any.
CardPool
The cards of one set that packs can hold, sorted the way packs are built: by color, then by rarity. Packs are one set plus one color, so each color of a set is its own pool.
CardPoolImporter
Reads card pools out of the MTGJSON build that DiscordPHP-MTG keeps, so the game has its own stable copy of every set it sells packs of.
ColorIdentity
A card's color identity (rule 903.4): its colors and the colors of the mana symbols in its cost and rules text. MTGJSON's `colorIdentity` is used when a card has it; older pools without it fall back to reading the cost and text.
CollectionPages
Pages of a player's collection, filtered by set, color, rarity and name.
CollectionQuery
A player's collection as a list to show: their cards with the copies they own, optionally narrowed to a set, a color, a rarity or part of a name, sorted by set, then color, then rarity (rarest first), then name.
DeckBuilder
Builds and edits a player's decks and side decks from the cards they own.
Draft
A booster draft event: a pod of players drafting packs of one set, then building decks and playing Swiss rounds with them.
DraftRules
How booster drafts run, from `config/drafts.php`.
DraftSeat
One player's seat at a draft: what they paid, the cards they have taken, the deck they build from them, and whether they have left the event or had their cards moved to their collection.
DraftService
Booster drafts, from sign-up to the last round.
MatchRewards
The points ranked games pay and the daily limits on them and on rental decks, from the `matches` part of `config/economy.php`.
PriceList
What cards and packs cost in points, and what selling a card pays, from the table in `config/economy.php`.
Receipt
What a purchase or a sale did: the cards, the points each line came to, and the player's points afterwards.
Shop
The points shop: players sell cards they don't want for points, and spend points on single cards or on packs of one color of one set.
Exporter
Decks and collections as files other programs open:
CardDefinition
What the rules need to know about a card, read from its pool data: its cost, types, power and toughness, keywords, mana abilities and the spell effects the engine understands.
Game
A two-player game of Magic under the Comprehensive Rules: the opening hands and London mulligan, the turn structure, priority and the stack, playing lands, casting spells with automatic mana payment, combat, triggered and activated abilities (loyalty and Equip included), tokens and state-based actions; and in Commander games, the command zone, commander tax and commander damage (rule 903).
GameException
An action the rules do not allow right now. The message says why and is shown to the player; the game is left as it was.
GameObject
One card in a game, in whatever zone it is in, with the state it has as a permanent: tapped, summoning sick, damage, counters and what it is attached to.
GamePlayer
One player in a game: life, poison, their own zones (library top last, hand, graveyard top last) and mana pool.
GameRecord
A game's record as plain text, laid out like a chess game score:
ManaCost
A mana cost as printed, e.g. `{2}{W}{U/B}{X}` (rule 202).
ManaPayer
Pays a cost for the player, the way MTG Arena does: from floating mana first, then by activating mana abilities of untapped permanents (rule 601.2g, which lets a player activate mana abilities while paying).
ManaPool
The mana a player has floating (rule 106.4). It empties at the end of every step and phase.
TextParser
Reads the parts of a card's rules text the engine applies:
Ladder
Each mode's ratings ladder, kept under `ladders/{mode}.json`. Ranked games (the ones matchmaking pairs) move both players' Elo ratings and add to their win, loss and draw record.
MatchRecord
A match between two players in one game mode: a challenge until the opponent accepts (or a ranked game matchmaking paired), then the game itself, and what each player has picked so far in their action panel (the spell they are casting and its targets, their blocks).
MatchService
Matches: direct challenges, the matchmaking queue and the games they start. Each player is in at most one live match (a challenge or a game) and at most one queue at a time.
PanelActions
What each control of a player's action panel does. A spell is cast in steps (pick the card and how to play it, then X, then each target), so the picks so far are kept on the match until the last one casts it. Abilities are activated the same way (pick the ability, then each target), and so are targets picked for a triggered ability. Attackers and blockers are picked first and declared with a button.
CardCounts
A multiset of card printings: MTGJSON uuid => how many copies.
Deck
A player's deck: a main deck and a side deck, built for one format.
Inventory
Every card a player owns, by MTGJSON printing uuid.
Player
A Discord user who plays: their profile, when they last opened their free daily pack, and the points they have to spend in the shop. Their cards are an {@see Inventory} and their decks are {@see Deck}s, each stored on its own.
GameMode
One way to play, from `config/modes.php`: the deck sizes it needs, how many copies of a card a deck may have, the starting life, and its card library (the sets it allows and the cards it bans).
GameModes
The game modes, one per deck format, read from `config/modes.php`.
Collection
`/collection [set] [color] [rarity] [name] [player]`: the cards a player owns, in pages, with a picker that opens any of them in DiscordPHP-MTG's card view.
Drafts
Booster drafts: `/draft create|join|start|leave|pack|pool|add|remove|lands|auto|ready|play|status`.
Exports
`/export deck [deck] [format]` and `/export collection [format] [set] [color] [rarity] [player]`: a player's decks and cards as a file for MTG Arena, Tabletop Simulator or Frogtown, sent as an attachment.
Matches
Matches between two players: `/match queue|challenge|board|leave|modes|ladder|log`, the challenge's **Accept** and **Decline**, the board's buttons, and each player's private action panel.
Menu
`/menu`, the home panel, and every panel's buttons, menus and forms (custom ids `pocket:ui:<owner>:…`; see {@see Panels}).
Packs
Free daily packs: `/pack open [set] [color]` and `/pack list`.
PlayerDecks
The player's own decks and side decks, built from the cards they own: `/decks list|show|create|add|remove|rename|format|commander|delete|use`, and the official decks anyone can borrow: `/decks rentals|rent`.
Quests
Daily and weekly quests: `/quests`.
Shop
The points shop: `/shop balance|price|buy-card|buy-pack|sell|sell-extras`.
Trades
Trades between players: `/trade offer|add|list|cancel`, and the **Accept**, **Decline** and **Cancel offer** buttons on an offer.
DailyPacks
One free pack a day per player. The day turns over at midnight UTC, so every player's next pack is ready at the same moment.
DailyPackUnavailableException
Thrown when a player has already opened today's free pack.
OpenedPack
A pack a player just opened, with what it added to their collection.
Pack
An opened pack: the cards of one color of one set, in the order they are revealed (commons first, the rare slot last).
PackGenerator
Builds packs of one color of one set.
Modal
A pop-up form: up to five labelled text boxes and menus. Its fields come back by their ids when it is sent; see {@see Panels::handle()}.
PanelResult
What a click or a sent form leads to: the panel to show, or a form to pop up, and a message for everyone in the channel (a challenge, a trade offer, a board) when the action concerns other players too.
Panels
Everything a player does, as panels of buttons, menus and forms: the home panel (`/menu`) and a panel for each part of the game. Slash commands open the same panels.
Pocket
The game's persistent state, all kept as JSON files under one directory:
Quest
One daily or weekly quest: do something a number of times for points.
QuestBook
The quests players can get, from `config/quests.php`.
Quests
Daily and weekly quests: each player gets a few of the {@see QuestBook}'s quests a day and a week, picked by a hash of their id and the period so they stay the same all day and differ between players. Progress is kept on the player and starts over when the period does; a quest pays its points the moment it is done.
RentalDeck
An official preconstructed deck players can borrow for a match without owning its cards. It carries its own card data, so it does not depend on which card pools are imported.
RentalDeckImporter
Reads a set's official preconstructed decks from DiscordPHP-MTG's MTGJSON build (its `setDecks` table) as rental decks, with the card data the rules engine needs.
Rentals
Rental decks: official preconstructed decks of the current sets that any player can play without owning the cards, a few games a day.
CardPoolRepository
Imported set pools, one JSON file each: `pools/{SETCODE}.json`.
DeckRepository
Player decks. All of a player's decks share one JSON file, `decks/{discordUserId}.json`, keyed by deck id, so listing them is one read.
DraftRepository
Draft events, one file each under `drafts/`, and the draft each player last joined, under `drafters/{playerId}.json`.
InventoryRepository
Player collections, one JSON file each: `inventories/{discordUserId}.json`.
MatchRepository
Matches, one file each under `matches/`, which live match each player is in, under `live/{playerId}.json`, the games each player has played under `history/{playerId}.json`, and who is waiting for a game in each mode, under `queues/{mode}.json`.
PlayerRepository
Players, one JSON file each: `players/{discordUserId}.json`.
RentalRepository
Rental decks, one JSON file each: `rentals/{id}.json`, written by `composer import-cards`.
TradeRepository
Open trade offers, one JSON file each: `trades/{id}.json`. An offer's file is deleted once it is accepted, declined, cancelled or expired, so the collection only ever holds open offers.
JsonStore
Documents kept as JSON files, one file per document: `{directory}/{collection}/{id}.json`.
StaleOfferException
Thrown when a player accepts an offer that has changed since they saw it.
TradeOffer
A trade one player offers another: cards and points they give, for cards and points they want back. The offer is open until the other player accepts or declines it, its sender cancels it, or it expires.
TradeService
Trades between players. One player offers cards and points for cards and points of another's; the other accepts or declines. Sending the offer is the sender's confirmation and accepting it is the other player's, so a trade happens only when both have agreed to the same revision of it.
