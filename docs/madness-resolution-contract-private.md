# Historical private Madness resolution contract and identity candidate

This note preserves the earlier partial-identity checkpoint and its original evidence. A subsequent private implementation uses the frozen #450 base and must be evaluated from its own current source/test evidence. Historical base was frozen #449 `3d035c8939d40a90676fc1a8fd18ee35f7eca925`; original tests-only checkpoint `d570bdd26b73ca850c1b8a311a099fc61d9d6fd1`. The historical scope below was not complete Madness resolution or a whole-card certificate.

## Required behavior

The [official September 2026 Comprehensive Rules](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.pdf), 702.35a–b and 608.2g, require the owner to be offered a cast while the Madness trigger resolves, without granting normal priority between that cast and the resolving ability's completion. If the player does not cast the card, its matching exile incarnation moves to its owner's graveyard. A countered trigger never creates a cast decision; its card remains in exile. The decision and all resulting actions refer to the original object and exile incarnation, even if a later incarnation has the same object ID and exile marker.

The private correction here addresses only the last identity boundary: an old trigger may bury its source only when its saved incarnation matches. It leaves the existing early-cast and automatic-burial behavior unchanged. The existing null-incarnation fallback is retained for callers without a supplied identity; normal resolving stack items supply one.

Four failures show early paid casts with the trigger still waiting (both seats, with/without JSON restore); two show automatic burial without a choice; two show casting permission remains after the trigger is countered. Assertions following each first failure were unreachable. The preceding `7cae043174e28083631a84a22a5961024a0880d0` run stopped at a test setup assumption and is retained as such.

The separate new paid-Voice identity probe seeded an exile → hand → exile transition on the discarded card, then resolved the original trigger. Before this candidate it failed for both seats: **2 tests /32 reached assertions /2 failures**, the new incarnation went to the graveyard. After the one-condition identity correction, the identity, countered-trigger controls and legacy control pass: **5 tests /89 assertions**. The seeded zone changes are a unit boundary, not a reanimation-card claim. The eight original timing failures are not claimed fixed.

## Proposed bounded resolution contract

1. A successfully resolving, noncountered Madness ability captures its own exact stack-entry ID, owner, source object and exile incarnation. A changed or missing source incarnation cannot be moved, offered, or rebound.
2. The owner receives one persisted, monotonic-ID decision to decline or attempt the actual Madness cast. The selected card, targets and cost must be validated before mutation; wrong player/ID, replay, illegal target and failed payment cannot spend twice or partially move a card. A card that cannot legally be cast can be declined and the matching incarnation is buried. A countered trigger creates no decision or permission.
3. On acceptance, use the normal cast legality, target pinning, mana and additional-cost semantics under an explicit resolution-only authorization tied to this decision, not global `exiledBy` or an artificial priority grant. Commit the actual spell entry and cast/target triggers; finish the original trigger continuation before settling or allowing priority.
4. If payment suspends for damage ordering, preserve the already-paid cast plus the original Madness resolution continuation in a bounded, validated saved state. Restore and resume without reselecting, re-pinning, repaying or losing queued triggers. A synchronous cast follows the same ordering. Decline and source departure cannot move a newer incarnation.
5. Panel/practice actions and older-save compatibility require explicit review. In particular, preserve schema21 when integrating #450 and the existing schema17/18 already-paid bare-target drain-or-reviewed-recovery prerequisite. Do not invent old incarnation identities or silently cancel/refund actions. No live storage was inspected.

## Concrete integration boundary

`Game::cast()` currently requires priority; `finishPaidCast()` calls `settle()`. `DamageResolution::validatePaidAction()` requires the paid caster to equal the priority player and validates a fixed cast-record shape; its paid-cast continuation has no outer resolving-trigger resume. Calling that path unchanged, temporarily assigning priority, or only removing the early exile permission can either expose priority/flush triggers too soon, lose a suspended outer continuation, or make all Madness casting unavailable. A safe implementation needs a narrowly typed resolving-cast context, explicit outer-resume validation and one completion seam across both synchronous and suspended payments. This may require a new Game save schema; none is selected or changed here.

Next review should cover that seam and additional meaningful regressions: both seats including an owner who does not have priority, cast/decline, unavailable mana or illegal targets, stale/wrong/replayed choices, source departure/re-entry, countered triggers, JSON at each phase, already-paid painful mana damage, exact original committed spell identity, remaining-resolution ordering, pending trigger release, panel/practice behavior and older-save refusal/migration. General cascade/suspend, arbitrary alternative costs and unrelated replacement/APNAP work remain outside this slice.

## Proposed serialized fields for review

Add a narrowly typed `optional_madness` choice with a fresh choice ID, owner seat, source object/exile incarnation, original resolving trigger ID, legal actions and the bounded existing `{item, steps, self, followed}` resolution resume. Validation must bind the resume item to the same Madness effect, owner, original trigger and source incarnation; check IDs against their next-ID counters; and refuse malformed, oversized, stale or replayed input before mutation. The current resolving item remains authoritative even when its owner is not the priority player. A source that left/re-entered must not be offered or moved by an older trigger.

For a Madness cast suspended by paid damage, introduce a distinct `madness_cast` continuation carrying the validated original resolving-trigger resume along with the already-paid cast's existing selected targets, payment and exact source incarnation. It must not be a generic priority-bypass flag. The completion dispatcher must finish the actual cast without settling, resume the original ability once, and only then settle if no further choice is pending. Synchronous casting follows the same ordering. A normal paid cast remains on its present validator and settle path. A failed validation/payment leaves the choice and its source unchanged; decline moves only the original exile incarnation.

Existing version20/21 saves have no such decision or `madness_cast` fields. Future loader changes should reject new pending fields under older versions; retain valid old queued trigger identities without deriving identity from `exiledBy` alone, and leave already-exiled cards in place when no trigger exists. Do not silently migrate ambiguous pending actions, cancel/refund them or read live match storage. The earlier schema17/18 paid bare-target hold remains. A new Game version and integration with #450's schema21 must be explicitly reviewed; this proposal makes no schema change.

`runSteps()` passes the saved item incarnation to `applyEffect()` and recursive effect applications propagate it. The direct production call that omits the argument constructs a Servo token in `fabricateServos()`; the other direct decision helpers pass their choice incarnation. No normal direct Madness invocation lacking identity was found. Reflection or malformed indirect input can still reach the null fallback; the identity-only correction retains that compatibility behavior and does not claim strict rejection of unknown identity.

An additional cached Mind Rot / Murderous Compulsion probe establishes the owner can differ from the priority player after an opponent's paid discard spell. It currently reaches the decision assertion and fails in both seats: **2 tests /16 reached assertions /2 failures** because resolution buries the card without a choice. Assertions following that failure are unreachable. This is separate from the already retained Voice baseline and seeded exile-reentry probe.
