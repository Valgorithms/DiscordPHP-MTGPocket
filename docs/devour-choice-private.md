This default-off local experiment addresses the cached ALA Thorn-Thrash Viashino's numeric Devour entry choice. It does not establish whole-card support, authorize deployment, or change any public PR. The cached fixture and intentionally failing pre-fix choice/observer baselines are retained separately. General Devour variants, simultaneous-entry replacement ordering, broad APNAP, transform/merged/copy interactions and other card abilities remain outside this slice.

See `devour-integration-matrix-private.md` for the source inventory and tested boundaries. No live match data was inspected or migrated.

## Bounded behavior

When enabled, a numeric Devour entrant waits for the controller to select zero or more original controlled creature incarnations before moving. Validated selections use the grouped sacrifice and death-observer path, and the chosen count supplies the entry counters before entry observers. A saved outer effect or permanent-spell tail is carried across later queued entry choices. Practice explicitly chooses none. Panels stage selections by page and submit the same engine-owned choice.

Only the reproduced paid-cast and reanimation paths, tested grouped observers, and specified queue and compatibility boundaries should be considered for review. Historical text recognition is not functional coverage, and no new whole-card certificate or aggregate recognition result is claimed.

The follow-up to local checkpoint `331ba36dbc92c2b3d632c1fa8a708725f89f7983` validates every queued schema-23 entry and each entry-state field consumed during entry before restoring the game or applying a Devour/land-entry answer. Invalid counters, stale/missing later entries, invalid flags and malformed delayed-exile contexts are refused without moving the selected creatures or changing the persisted match. Counter arithmetic is bounded before mutation. This validates structural compatibility; it does not authenticate arbitrary forged saves or claim general replacement/entry handling.
