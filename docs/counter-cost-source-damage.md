# Damage paid with a counter from the source

The complete cached Ancient Hydra and Monoskelion records have abilities that pay mana and remove a counter from the source, then say “It deals” damage. The existing activated-ability parser understood both costs and the damage effect but normalized that source pronoun only after self-sacrifice, so these abilities were unread. It now recognizes the pronoun for unambiguous self-counter costs with optional mana or tap costs. Costs involving another creature remain unread rather than assigning an ambiguous source.

On main `e5a0d1c94c78db07efb2ec5e242cf478f2936ad1`, four paid casts and attempted activations reached 20 assertions and all failed because the ability was missing. The expanded corrected suite passes 21 tests and 198 assertions across both seats: actual entry counters, payment before resolution, saved stack, prevention, source returned to hand, last-counter behavior and atomic invalid target or missing-cost rejection. The existing fading test's assertion that Hydra's clause was unread is updated; its other unread controls remain.
