# Mana-value-limited permanent targets

A transformed Huntmaster of the Fells has front-face mana value four, but five existing permanent-target filters read its back face's absent mana cost as zero. As a result, paid Eliminate could illegally target it. Those battlefield filters now use the existing `battlefieldManaValue()` helper. [Wizards' rules notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/eldritch-moon-release-notes-2016-07-08) describe the front-face mana-value rule. Graveyard-card and stack-spell filters are unchanged.

The baseline on `3ff9ce385106af573de73d73b25d4831981c3829` reached 128 assertions across 16 cases and failed 12: all five affected filters and the illegal paid cast for both seats. Four legal and revalidation controls passed. The corrected expanded suite passes 20 cases and 166 assertions, including legal face-down targets, controller restrictions, atomic rejection, saved-stack resolution, and revalidation after a seeded face-up characteristic change. Huntmaster's transformation and the face-up transition are seeded, not a claim about their abilities.
