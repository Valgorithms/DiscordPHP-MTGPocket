This private slice is based on main `570771053d31f16d700178d9fbc742da37e9106d`. It does not integrate or modify #444, #445 or #446. It corrects independently reproduced stack-entry identity bugs and preserves the still-incorrect upfront Ward policy as an explicit characterization. ## Reproduced behavior and bounded correction

On unchanged main, the two maintained initial probes reached five assertions and failed twice: the existing counter primitive moved a tapped Prodigal Pyromancer from battlefield to graveyard when countering its activation, and moved the original Shock when countering its ability-shaped spell copy. The baseline tests and normalized source hashes are archived outside the checkout with their runner receipts. An earlier baseline also emitted a test-only dynamic-property deprecation; the clean repeat removes that unused property and still reproduces both failures. Later test-construction and implementation replay failures remain separate receipts.

The cached complete Dog Walker fixture is identical to #446's selected record, UUID `7037c6a6-2329-5e62-a365-ddd83f37d0aa`, fixture SHA256 `27a09d399cdd6cbe3e819319bd47c28c0b08295bc635020daa55f2f4fcca973c`. The paid disguise/targeting characterization on both seats passes 2 tests / 28 assertions: an unaffordable upfront Ward rejects atomically; three mana buys Shock plus Ward immediately, with one stack entry and no Ward choice. This documents a remaining gap, not correct Ward timing. Its first replay assertion lost JSON float precision; the corrected harness explicitly preserves zero fractions.

`counterStackItem(stackId, exile)` now returns the exact removed entry or null for missing, stale-card or uncounterable entries. Card spells still follow existing graveyard/exile/flashback routing. Abilities and ability-shaped copies remove only their own stack entries, even after their source leaves or changes incarnation. An uncounterable spell copy carries a boolean characteristic snapshot, preserved and type-checked on restore; an ordinary ability does not inherit uncounterability from its source. Old entries without this optional field retain their prior default. An older executable drops this new copy flag; rollback cannot preserve that characteristic. Historic already-saved uncounterable copies without a snapshot cannot be retrospectively certified. Save authentication and general copy architecture remain outside scope.

The ordinary counter effect delegates only removal to this helper. Its existing automatic counter-unless-payment policy, uncounterable guard and followed-controller context remain. The new primitive probes do not certify Stifle parsing or public ability-counter target kinds.

## Proposed typed Ward contract (not implemented)

The existing September 25, 2026 Comprehensive Rules are authoritative: 702.21a, 113.2c, 113.7a, 118.12a, 603.3a-b, 608.2g and 707.10. Source: <https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.pdf>. Reuse the cached Dog Walker selection; no corpus import is needed.

Proposed fixed costs are a closed union: `{kind: mana, symbols: string}`, `{kind: life, amount: int}` and, only with explicit card selection, `{kind: discard, count: 1}`. Dynamic X and arbitrary sacrifices remain unsupported. Genuine static targeting taxes are a separate representation, applied only to casting costs. A Frost Titan-style clause referring to the offending item's controller must retain that wording's payer rule; it is not silently normalized to Ward's event-player reference.

Each observed instance is `{instanceKey, source: [objectId, incarnation], controller, label, cost, payerRule}`. The key identifies a printed ability index or a granting effect's source/incarnation/index. Collect intrinsic, face-down, attachment, anthem and temporary grants before keyword deduplication. Identical Ward instances remain separate; one spell naming the same permanent in several target slots does not multiply one instance.

The targeting-event snapshot is `{eventId, stackId, stackController, newTargets: [pinnedObjectReference], instances: [instance]}`. Capture it read-only when validated targets become established, before costs can remove a Ward source, and publish only on successful action commitment. Stack copies get their own event/stack identity. Future retargeting compares prior/new targets and does not retrigger on an unchanged target. No legality decision or event creation may partially mutate the game on failure.

A pending Ward trigger has the ordinary source/incarnation/controller fields, `kinds=[]`, `targets=[]`, and a closed internal effect holding the event's stack reference, instance and payer. That reference is not a Magic target. Source departure, blink, ability loss or later control changes cannot erase the old trigger or redirect it to a new source incarnation. A missing referenced stack entry makes countering a no-op, rather than countering another entry; the optional non-targeted payment effect still resolves.

At resolution, propose a `ward_payment` choice containing `{id, seat, triggerStackId, referencedStackId, instanceKey, cost, options, resume}`. Keep a distinct payer seat and trigger controller: existing optional choices often equate them, which Ward cannot do. Validate all identities, exact option, bounded cost shape, selected cards and complete payment plan before mutation. Decline is always available. After a committed payment, append a closed completion step and never recompute/pay the cost on replay. Only that completion may attempt the counter. Serialize only engine-owned data, not callbacks or method names; reject malformed/stale/repeated answers atomically. This contract does not authenticate externally forged saves.

## Controller ordering and frozen-lane dependencies

`flushTriggers()` currently groups APNAP but uses deterministic insertion order within a controller. Proper multiple Ward costs require the controller to choose that group's stack order; mixed simultaneous Ward and non-Ward triggers also matter. A bounded pending-group ordering decision needs stable trigger identities, a permutation of the exact current group, wrong-seat/stale checks and save/replay. Ordering only Ward while ignoring the controller's other simultaneous triggers is not a full solution. General multiplayer/APNAP redesign is outside this foundation.

Ordinary trigger/payment preparation can proceed on main. #444/#445 overlap at `pay()`, counter-unless handling and saved resolution completion. Their specific contribution is suspension for painful mana and nested graveyard/Recover triggers. A future Ward payment must compose with the reviewed paid-resolution continuation, carry its still-pending counter completion, and not resume any outer spell before completion. Existing paid legacy Ward tails must not be refunded or synthesized again. Allocate any future schema version against the integration tree; this foundation adds only an optional typed copy-characteristic field, not a Ward pending-state schema.

Before implementation, review the fixed-cost/ordering boundary and public ability-counter target-kind scope. Preserve historical recognition and existing evidence; update upfront-tax tests deliberately only when the corresponding gameplay behavior changes.
