# Animated planeswalker damage

A paid Lightning Bolt exposed a damage bookkeeping defect when its target was both a creature and a planeswalker: damage was marked on the creature, but loyalty was not removed. An indestructible animated planeswalker could consequently remain on the battlefield at what should have been zero loyalty. The engine now applies both applicable consequences before state-based actions. Infect or wither uses the existing creature counter behavior alongside loyalty loss. Prevention still happens before either consequence. The official [Gideon rules FAQ](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/frequently-asked-questions-2013-01-21) describes both consequences and the zero-loyalty result.

The baseline on main `570771053d31f16d700178d9fbc742da37e9106d` reached 64 assertions in six tests: four animated cases failed for the two seats; ordinary planeswalker controls passed. The focused correction passed 14 tests and 148 assertions. They use complete cached M10 Lightning Bolt (`78380203-7623-50bc-ab48-577cd655ee9a`) and BFZ Gideon, Ally of Zendikar (`5ae34679-cf9a-5de0-a03f-aeb166372c7f`) records. The normalized fixture SHA-256 is `7762e84fd91d5e1f8f95f139e73aa3c2e38ccdeca8dbb6a9c9a955b3bc20904b`; the source pool hashes are M10 `8d3ac05dce2c6877cd38dccc987e4a82692488cd4f9ea4b497ab977a69642aa0` and BFZ `4b46e3eac199ec81c6270e6b492e35df0acda086ba48efb79b50305cfb09aac1`.

The tests seed Gideon's animation and indestructibility rather than activating its printed ability. The unpreventable cases isolate damage despite its printed prevention; the partial and shield-prevention cases instead model a hypothetical animation without that printed prevention. They also exercise a synthetic infect combat source, a target returned to hand before resolution, and JSON restore. This does not certify Gideon's abilities, general damage replacement ordering, battles, newest printings, or any whole card. Recognition totals remain historical. The independent held damage-continuation stack also contains the prior bookkeeping and will need this correction integrated and revalidated before its own merge; this branch does not alter that stack or any save schema.
