# Infest and Pyroclasm: paid all-creature effects

This evidence-only addition uses the complete cached CLU Infest record (`a4683dd0-caf1-50aa-926c-8d09b1d0bded`) and DSK Pyroclasm record (`aa3cdc04-b8f4-52a6-867c-81478d8accd4`) from the existing `tests/Fixtures/state-based-deaths.json`, normalized-LF SHA-256 `a2b90b3b251a714c4b86c5f94c92e54ef497f01572ae951afe679d2e302879d3`. These spells previously enabled selected simultaneous state-based death-observer tests; their effect distinctions were not independently recorded in the ledger. No data was imported or downloaded.

The existing engine passed the paid baselines separately: Infest 2 cases / 30 assertions and Pyroclasm 2 / 32. Both seats pay the printed costs, affect each player's creatures and restore the pending spell through JSON. Infest reduces power and toughness without dealing damage or consuming prevention, can put an indestructible creature into the graveyard at zero toughness, and expires at cleanup. Pyroclasm deals damage, consumes a prevention shield, leaves an indestructible creature with marked damage, and clears damage at cleanup. Paid Counterspell responses are tested for both spells and seats after JSON restoration. No production correction is claimed.

Only these cached printings and enumerated paths are evidenced. Synthetic creature controls and Counterspell are not independently certified. These tests do not certify newest printings, general simultaneous damage, layers, replacement effects, APNAP, all card interactions or live Discord play. Historical recognition counts and whole-card certificates remain unchanged.
